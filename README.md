# Seller Growth & Marketplace Health 

## Project Overview

The **Seller Growth & Marketplace Health Assessment** project analyzes seller performance, marketplace GMV concentration, customer support issues, and seller incentive effectiveness. The goal is to identify growth opportunities, highlight data-quality limitations, and support data-driven investment decisions in a seller marketplace.

## Objectives

- Analyze seller GMV and marketplace concentration.
- Segment sellers based on GMV ranking and business importance.
- Examine seller support tickets and customer experience issues.
- Evaluate seller program costs against recorded incremental GMV.
- Develop a ₹35 crore investment allocation strategy.
- Build interactive Power BI dashboards for marketplace insights.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Excel:** Data cleaning, analysis, seller ranking, segmentation, and investment modeling.
- **MySQL:** SQL queries, joins, aggregations, CTEs, and window functions.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, KPI cards, and business visualizations.
- **Python & Pandas:** Data analysis and data manipulation where applicable.

## Dataset

The project uses seller marketplace data covering:

- Sellers
- Seller Products
- Orders
- Seller Performance
- Seller Tickets
- Seller Payouts
- Seller Programs
- Category Market

The datasets support seller-level analysis of GMV, commissions, support issues, program costs, and incremental GMV.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Cleaning:** Identified duplicates, missing values, inconsistent labels, and data-quality issues.
2. **Excel Analysis:** Calculated seller GMV, seller rankings, concentration, segmentation, and program efficiency.
3. **MySQL Analysis:** Used joins, CTEs, aggregations, and window functions to analyze seller performance and marketplace trends.
4. **Power BI Visualization:** Developed four dashboards to communicate findings.
5. **Investment Modeling:** Proposed a ₹35 crore allocation across major seller-growth interventions.
6. **Data Strategy:** Defined seller master-data governance, seller health scoring, supply-demand measurement, incentive experimentation, and onboarding and quality controls.

## Power BI Dashboards

### 1. Marketplace Health
Provides an overview of marketplace performance and seller GMV.

### 2. Seller Concentration
Analyzes seller rankings and the share of GMV contributed by top sellers.

### 3. Seller Quality & Customer Experience
Examines support-ticket volume, issue categories, escalations, and ticket resolution time.

### 4. Incentive ROI & Seller Programs
Compares seller-program costs with recorded incremental GMV and evaluates program efficiency.

## Key Findings

- **Total GMV analyzed:** ₹28,797,750.63.
- **Top 10 seller GMV:** Approximately ₹930,910.86.
- **Top 10 GMV concentration:** Approximately 2.71% of total GMV.
- **Seller program cost:** Approximately ₹370,047.93.
- **Recorded incremental GMV:** Approximately ₹591,893.71.
- **Incremental GMV per rupee of program cost:** Approximately 1.60x.

*Note: The program efficiency ratio is not profit ROI and does not prove causal impact. The calculated take rate and repeated seller-performance metrics require further validation.*

## Business Recommendations

- Improve seller master-data quality and metric definitions.
- Monitor GMV concentration and seller dependency.
- Investigate recurring customer support issues.
- Evaluate incentives using controlled experiments.
- Strengthen seller onboarding and product quality controls.
- Validate financial metrics before making investment decisions.
